What you get

Everything in one dashboard

No more juggling texts, paper invoices, and memory. Keep everything organized in one place.

Job management

Create jobs, attach client vehicles, set status (Pending → In Progress → Complete), and track every job through your pipeline.

Vehicle view

See the full maintenance history of any customer vehicle before you even open the hood. Fewer surprises, better service.

Client messaging

Message car owners directly in the app. Send updates, ask questions, and share estimates — no phone tag required.

Reviews & ratings

Customers you have worked with can leave reviews on your profile. Build a reputation that attracts new customers automatically.

Directory listing

Get listed in the mechanic directory. Car owners in your area searching for a mechanic can find you, see your reviews, and message you directly.

Shareable profile

Every mechanic gets a shareable profile link. Put it on your website, your business cards, or anywhere customers might look you up.

How it works

Up and running in minutes

1

Create your free account

Sign up with your email. No credit card needed to create your account.

2

Set up your mechanic profile

Add your shop name, specialties, location, and contact info. Your profile goes live in the mechanic directory immediately.

3

Bring in your past customers

Upload your old service records from a spreadsheet, then invite every customer in one tap. See how to import your records.

4

Start managing jobs

Create your first job, attach a customer vehicle, and start messaging your client — all from the dashboard.

5

Collect reviews & grow

As clients leave reviews, your profile ranking improves. More visibility means more customers finding you organically.
